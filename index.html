ORIBERRY AS A DEVELOPMENT SOLUTION

Oriberry is Vietnamese Social Enterprise. We and our farmers fight poverty together by producing great Vietnamese coffee and tea. From the experience of working in many charity and non-for-profit projects since 1998 to help indigenous and ethnic minorities people in remote areas of Vietnam, Oriberry was found in 2010 as a more sustainable development solution through creating direct-trade | fair-trade coffee and tea products. Our goal is to create more jobs for smallholders and provide excellent service to our customers both online and in our shops.
